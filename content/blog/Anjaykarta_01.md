## Soft-Launching My Own TTRPG System: Anjaykarta!

**What is Anjaykarta?** I like to define Anjaykarta as a hack of _Electric Bastionland_ combined with _Blades in the Dark_'s clock-driven faction system. It's a fast-paced, low-prep urban roleplaying game designed to capture the gritty, chaotic, and hyper-capitalist survival experience of a fictionalized Jakarta.

## The Standout Features

- **Finding "Walking Wallets" as a Goal:** Similar to EBL, our players need to settle their debt. The difference? Your group is saddled with a massive, shared debt of exactly 100 million Rupiah. The only way to survive is to hunt down elusive "Walking Wallets" wealthy benefactors and Sugar Daddies who have enough capital to wipe your ledger clean before your time runs out.
- **Hinges Around a Core Triangle:** Every decision forces a continuous trade-off between **Time** ( **Money** (tracked in abstract 10k "Ceban" increments), and **Safety** (a physical step-die that degrades from a d10 down to a deadly d4 when taking risks).
- **Concrete Pointcrawl:** There are no grid maps here. Travel is abstracted across five massive City States, each governed by Wealth, Danger, and Traffic stats. Navigating to a building means rolling on a _Kecamatan_ table to instantly generate local street flavor.
- **Streamlined Three-Tier Economy:** Everything from weapons and clinics to black markets operates on an intuitive 1-to-3 tier system. Tier 3 is cheap street-level grit, while Tier 1 is high-end luxury heavily gatekept by wealth or faction influence.
- **Factions as a Living City:** Players interact with local _Ormas_ and syndicates, ticking _Compliance_ clocks for favors or _Heat_ clocks when they inevitably choose violence.

## Why make Anjaykarta?

I have three main reasons why I ultimately decided to create this system.

**1. A Gateway for My Local Friends** 

I really love inviting my friends into my hobbies, but TTRPGs can have a tough barrier to entry. You need a dedicated group, a Dungeon Master, specific dice, and hours just to learn the rules. Most people recognize _Dungeons & Dragons_ as the entry point, but it has a massive learning curve and a Eurocentric fantasy setting that not everyone resonates with. I wanted to reduce this cognitive load. What better way to get immersed quickly than using your own city? Anjaykarta was made so Indonesians can recognize the details instantly. Even the "Failed Career" options—like the _Calon CPNS_ or the _Mantan Jukir Liar_—reflect everyday Indonesian life. I hope players can step into the shoes of everyday people and see Jakarta through a new lens.

**2. Introducing Foreigners to the Real Jakarta**

Let's be honest: Indonesia's cultural export is usually either incredibly weird (like the Tung Tung Sahur memes) or completely obscure. There is a massive gap in how the rest of the world sees us. I want this TTRPG to act as a pavement for foreigners to truly experience what it's like to navigate this city. Instead of generic fantasy taverns, they get to experience the sheer chaos, the sprawling traffic, and the vibrant street-food culture of a hyper-capitalist concrete circus.

**3. Discovering the City for Myself** 

I grew up here, but I haven't really gotten the chance to explore the depths of Jakarta when I was little. Now that I have the freedom, building this game is my excuse to dive deep into the city's veins. From researching the elite hangouts in Jakarta Selatan to the heavy container truck traffic of Jakarta Utara, writing this system is my way of learning more about Jakarta and, ultimately, learning to love it for all its flaws.

## What's next?

The system is *far* from done. Currently, I'm writing both the English and Indonesian version, so doing double the work is very tiring. Moreover, I myself am also still refining the Faction and misc system in Jaykarta. We plan to fully release somewhere in 2027, so until then, we'll be posting updates and WIPs in our itch and social media. Thank you for reading!
